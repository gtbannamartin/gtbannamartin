## What I build

I ship production AI systems for the communities existing infrastructure has been slowest to serve.

- **[PathwayAI](https://djmpinstitute.org/pathwayai)** — a live five-agent agentic platform (Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers) monitoring ten federal workforce databases and matching Chicago residents to technology career pathways. Web, mobile, and SMS delivery.
- **TCAF™ — Trustworthy Civic AI Framework** — my doctoral research: a four-pillar governance architecture (Secure by Architecture · Ethical by Design · Equitable by Intent · Scalable by Design) for evaluating agentic AI in high-stakes civic deployments.
- **AccessBridge** *(in development)* — disability-inclusive civic navigation, a sibling TCAF instantiation to PathwayAI.
- **AI education at scale** — four-year AI/STEM instructor for CSUN's BroSE residential leadership program; 13 years directing STEM at Rainbow PUSH Coalition; 500+ students served through DJMP.
